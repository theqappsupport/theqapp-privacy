TheQApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

TheQApp is a Quran application that provides Quran reading, memorization, audio recitation, and related features.

Information and Permissions

The app may request location permission to support location-dependent features such as prayer location and Qibla direction. Camera access may be used for optional augmented-reality Qibla features. You can deny these permissions, although related features may not work fully.

Audio and Offline Content

The app may download Quran recitation audio and other content to your device for playback and offline use.

Internet and Third-Party Services

Internet access may be used to retrieve online content and services. When you use these features, relevant information may be processed by the service providers involved. Please review their privacy policies where applicable.

Data Storage and Sharing

The app does not require you to create an account to use its core Quran features. Information stored locally by the app may remain on your device. We do not sell your personal information. This policy should be updated if analytics, advertising, or other data-collecting services are added.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Security and Changes

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated as the app evolves.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact provided on TheQApp's Google Play listing.
